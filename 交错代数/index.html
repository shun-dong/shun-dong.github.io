[!definition]
symmetric 对称性. $f(\dots uv\dots)=f(\dots vu\dots)$
alternating 交错性. $f(\cdots vv \cdots) = 0$
skew-symmetric 反对称性. $f(\cdots u_i u_j \cdots) = -f(\cdots u_j u_i \cdots)$
If we change the succession condition to arbitrary condition, we call it complete.

[!proposition]
symmetric $\iff$ complete symetric
alternating $\iff$ Skew-symmetric $\iff$ complete skew-symmetric $\iff$ complete alternating

$Proof.$ (alternating $\implies$ skew-symmetric)
$$\begin{align} 0&=f(\dots u+v,u+v\dots)\\&=f(\dots uu\dots)+f(\dots uv\dots)+f(\dots vu\dots)+f(\dots vv\dots) \\&=f(\dots uv\dots)+f(\dots vu\dots) \end{align}$$
(Skew-symmetric $\implies$ complete skew-symmetric)
$$\begin{align} f(\dots v_i\dots v_j\dots)&=(-1)^{j-i}f(\dots v_jv_i\dots)=(-1)^{j-i}(-1)^{j-(i+1)}f(\dots v_j\dots v_i\dots)\\&=-f(\dots v_g\dots v_i\dots) \end{align}$$
(Complete skew-symmetric $\implies$ complete alternating)
$$f(…v \dots v \dots)=f(\dots 0 \dots v \dots)+f(\dots v \dots 0 \dots)=0$$
(Complete alternating $\implies$ alternating) trivial
$\blacksquare$

[!definition] K-algebra
$(R,+,\cdot_K,\circ)$

  1. K is a field
  2. $(R,+,\cdot_K)$ is a vector space
  3. $(R, +, \circ)$ is a ring
  4. the ring and the vector space are compatible i.e. m is a bilinear map.

$$ \tilde A(T)=\sum_{i_1,i_2\dots i_k~are~different} T^{i_1i_2\dots i_k}e_{i_1}\wedge e_{i_2}\dots\wedge e_{i_k} $$
$$ =\sum_{i_1<i_2\dots<i_k}\sum_{\sigma\in\mathfrak S_k}\text{sgn}(\sigma) T^{i_{\sigma(1)}i_{\sigma(2)}\dots i_{\sigma(k)}}e_{i_1}\wedge e_{i_2}\dots\wedge e_{i_k} $$
$$ \implies \left< e_{i_1}\wedge e_{i_2}\dots\wedge e_{i_k}|i_1<i_2\dots<i_k \right>=\bigwedge V $$ (Independent)
If $\sum_{i_1<i_2\dots<i_k} T^{i_1i_2\dots i_k}e_{i_1}\wedge e_{i_2}\dots\wedge e_{i_k}=0$,
$$ \iff \sum_{i_1<i_2\dots<i_k} T^{i_1i_2\dots i_k}e_{i_1}\otimes e_{i_2}\dots \otimes e_{i_k} \in I$$
But $e_{i_2}\dots \otimes e_{i_k}$ do not have duplicated indices. $\implies T^{i_1i_2\dots i_k}e_{i_1}=0$

[!proposition]
$V = U \oplus W$, $\bigwedge^k V = \bigoplus_{r+s=k} \bigwedge^r U \otimes \bigwedge^s W$

$Proof.$ 设 $\dim U=m$, $\dim W=n$, 取基 $\{u_1,\dots,u_m\}$ 和 $\{w_1,\dots,w_n\}$, 则它们的并是 $V$ 的一组基.

$\bigwedge^k V$ 的基为 $\{e_{i_1}\wedge\cdots\wedge e_{i_k}\mid 1\leq i_1<\cdots<i_k\leq m+n\}$。每个基向量可写为 $r$ 个 $u$ 型向量与 $s=k-r$ 个 $w$ 型向量的外积：
$$ u_{a_1}\wedge\cdots\wedge u_{a_r}\wedge w_{b_1}\wedge\cdots\wedge w_{b_s} $$
其中 $1\leq a_1<\cdots<a_r\leq m$, $1\leq b_1<\cdots<b_s\leq n$。

考虑线性映射
$$\phi_{r,s}: \bigwedge^r U \otimes \bigwedge^s W \to \bigwedge^k V,\quad (u_{a_1}\wedge\cdots\wedge u_{a_r})\otimes(w_{b_1}\wedge\cdots\wedge w_{b_s})\mapsto u_{a_1}\wedge\cdots\wedge u_{a_r}\wedge w_{b_1}\wedge\cdots\wedge w_{b_s}$$
它将基映为基中不同的元素，且值域彼此不交（因 $r$ 不同则 $u$ 的个数不同），故 $\phi_{r,s}$ 是单射。全体 $\{\operatorname{im}\phi_{r,s}\}_{r+s=k}$ 生成的正是 $\bigwedge^k V$ 的所有基向量，故
$$\bigwedge^k V = \bigoplus_{r+s=k} \operatorname{im}\phi_{r,s} \cong \bigoplus_{r+s=k} \bigwedge^r U \otimes \bigwedge^s W$$

维数验证：$\dim\bigwedge^k V=\binom{m+n}{k}=\sum_{r+s=k}\binom{m}{r}\binom{n}{s}=\sum_{r+s=k}\dim(\bigwedge^r U\otimes\bigwedge^s W)$。
$\blacksquare$
